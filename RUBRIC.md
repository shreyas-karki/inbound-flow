# Thinking Math! Inbound Lead Rubric

| Level | Name | Share of sample | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0** | Dead end | ~12% (25 of 200) | Auto thank-you with free resources. No Slack post, no Salesforce task. |
| **1** | Long shot | ~55% (110 of 200) | Auto email with digital samples and one ask: "Who on your team chooses math curriculum?" No proactive BDR touch. |
| **2** | Discovery | ~17% (35 of 200) | BDR calendar on the thank-you page, plus a 3-touch BDR sequence. |
| **3** | First Touch | ~7% (15 of 200) | Growth manager calendar on the thank-you page, physical samples, Slack alert to the growth manager. |

Before any of this, existing customers and open deals (on the school or its parent district) go straight to their owner.

## Level 0: Dead end
Anyone who will never buy a district curriculum.
- Parents and homeschoolers
- Students, or anyone whose message gives them away as one
- Vendors, sponsorship pitches and conference invites
- Press, politicians, universities and job seekers
- High-school-only teachers (no K-8 grades)
- Anyone outside the United States and Canada
- Internal tests and nonsense submissions

**Why:** none of the ~25 in the sample produced a meeting, and each Slack post costs a BDR a read.

## Level 1: Long shot
A real K-8 educator with no buying power and no stated reason to switch now.
- Teachers and interventionists whose message shows no evaluation, review or pilot (coaches and TOSAs never land here)
- At a district that is not Hot or Warm in Starbridge
- Unknown or "Other" roles with no message to place them

Never at a district of $500k+ Total PARR: those leads go to Level 2 instead.

Replies go to the BDR inbox. A reply that names a decision maker or describes a review re-scores the lead.

**Why:** 0 of 5 meetings held with teachers who gave no evaluation signal advanced. A teacher's answer to the adoption question is not reliable on its own.

## Level 2: Discovery
A real chance where we don't yet have the decision maker or the WHY NOW, so a BDR goes and gets them.
- Champions without an active review: math/STEM director or coordinator, curriculum coordinator, MTSS coordinator, Title I director, SpEd director, assistant principal
- Decision makers with no WHY NOW at a district that isn't Hot or Warm
- Principals outside NYC who lead one building of a larger district, unless they have a WHY NOW
- Instructional coaches, math coaches and TOSAs, even with no signal: they can always book a BDR call
- Teachers who write that their school is evaluating, previewing, piloting or reviewing, or whose district is Hot or Warm
- Anyone who would otherwise be Level 1 at a very large district ($500k+ Total PARR). A district that size always enters the funnel, even through a teacher.

The call has two goals: the decision maker's name and the WHY NOW. With both, the BDR books a First Touch with the growth manager.

**Why:** champions brought in our largest inbound deals (Burlington $200k, Oak Park 97, CESA 4). The teachers and coaches who booked qualified meetings (Kingman, Hainesport) all said they were evaluating.

## Level 3: First Touch
The person who signs, or the person running the review, with a reason to change now.
- Every curriculum director at a district of $30k+ Total PARR (650+ students in Starbridge when Salesforce has no PARR), with or without a WHY NOW
- Every curriculum director adopting in SY26-27 or SY27-28, at any district size or with no size data. Both kinds skip Claude, and neither the floor nor a low Starbridge score caps them
- Other decision makers (superintendent, assistant superintendent, CAO, curriculum director, director of teaching & learning, district administrator) AND any of:
  - adopting in SY26-27 or SY27-28
  - a Hot or Warm Starbridge district, or an account score of 70+
  - "I'm not sure" on adoption at a district with Strong or Ideal fit
  - a message saying they are searching, reviewing, piloting, or asking for pricing
  - met us in person (Lunch & Learn, PD, conference)
- Champions running an active review, such as a math coordinator who writes "we're starting a 6-8 review"
- Instructional coaches, math coaches and TOSAs at a Hot or Warm district (or score 70+), with or without an adoption date. At a Hot district of 5,000+ students adopting SY26-27/27-28 they skip Claude and go straight to the GM
- Anyone, including a teacher, whose message says they sit on the curriculum, adoption or pilot committee, or lead the review
- Principals who decide for their whole school: NYC principals, and principals of a single-school district, charter or private school

**Floor:** a district under $30k Total PARR in Salesforce, or with a Starbridge score under 50, never gets a First Touch, however senior the person or strong the signal. Those leads become a Level 2 Discovery with the BDR. Size is measured by PARR; when Salesforce has no PARR, under 650 students in Starbridge (about $30k) counts instead. With no data at all the floor doesn't apply. The one exception is curriculum directors. At a $30k+ district they keep the First Touch whatever the Starbridge score, and when adopting in SY26-27 or SY27-28 they keep it at any size.

**Why curriculum directors:** Kearny, NJ (Nov 2): a curriculum director at $209,720 PARR adopting SY28-29 got a BDR Discovery for lack of a WHY NOW. The team wants every curriculum director at a $30k+ district on a growth manager's calendar. Since Nov 9, every curriculum director adopting soon books on the calendar directly too, whatever the district's size.

**Why $30k:** of 763 US First Touches since Aug 2025, districts at $20–30k PARR won 2 of 34; districts at $30–40k won 7 of 42, as well as any larger band.

If no meeting is booked within 48 hours, the growth manager follows up personally.

**Why:** about 15 leads fit this profile in the sample and only 2 booked (Simsbury, Nenana). The rest waited 1–3 weeks in the BDR queue.

## Signals, bumps and tie-breakers
The free-text message is the strongest signal, so it can override the role dropdown in either direction.

| Signal | Effect |
|---|---|
| Message says evaluating, previewing, reviewing, searching, piloting, or asks for pricing | WHY NOW. Teacher or coach → Level 2; decision maker or champion → Level 3 |
| Coach or TOSA at a Hot or Warm district | Level 3 |
| Message shows a student, parent, vendor or nonsense | Level 0, whatever role was picked |
| Adoption answer SY26-27 / SY27-28 | Counts only from decision makers and champions. A teacher's or coach's answer needs a second signal |
| 2+ people from the same district within 30 days | Up one level |
| Met us in person (Lunch & Learn, PD, conference) | Up one level; end users still cap at Level 2 |
| Starbridge Blocked, or the district just committed to another core program | Down one level |
| Form role and Salesforce role disagree | Use the more senior one, and flag it for cleanup |

- Teachers reach Level 3 only through committee membership or leading the review. Coaches and TOSAs also reach it through a hot district.
- Decision maker, champion or coach, torn between 2 and 3 → 3.
- End user, torn between 1 and 2 → 1, unless there is an evaluation signal or a hot district.
- Every decision carries a one-line reason under 25 words naming the deciding factor.

## How we'll know it works (weekly for the first month)

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Level 3 leads with a meeting booked within 7 days | Most of them; baseline 2 of ~15 |
| Level 2 leads with a first BDR touch within 2 business days | All of them |
| Inbounds sent to a BDR on a district with a live deal | 0; baseline ~7% |
| Level 1 replies that re-score to Level 2 or 3 | Tracked, no target yet |
