FirstExisting customers and open deals

Go straight to the deal owner, then the account owner, on the school or its parent district. No calendar. Needs a Salesforce integration user from RevOps; until then they are scored like new leads.

L0Dead end

Anyone who will never buy a district curriculum:

  • parents and homeschoolers, students
  • vendors, sponsorship and conference pitches
  • press, politicians, universities, job seekers
  • high-school-only teachers (no K-8 grades)
  • anyone outside the US and Canada, nonsense

L1Long shot

A real K-8 educator with no buying power and no reason to switch now:

  • teachers and interventionists with no evaluation, review or pilot signal, at a district that isn't Hot or Warm
  • "Other" roles with nothing to place them

A teacher's adoption answer alone doesn't lift them. Coaches and TOSAs never land here.

L2Discovery with the BDR

A real chance, but without the decision maker or the why-now yet:

  • champions without an active review: math/STEM director or coordinator, curriculum coordinator, MTSS, Title I, SpEd director, assistant principal
  • decision makers with no why-now at a district that isn't Hot or Warm
  • principals of one building in a larger district (outside NYC)
  • instructional coaches, math coaches and TOSAs, always, even with no signal
  • teachers who say their school is evaluating, previewing or piloting, or whose district is Hot or Warm
  • any Level 3 that falls under the floor

The call has two goals: the decision maker's name and the why-now. With both, the BDR books a First Touch.

L3First Touch with the GM

  • decision makers (superintendent, assistant superintendent, CAO, curriculum director, director of T&L, district administrator) with any why-now: adopting SY26-27 or SY27-28; a Hot or Warm district or score 70+; "I'm not sure" at a Strong or Ideal fit district; a message about searching, reviewing, piloting or pricing
  • champions running an active review
  • coaches and TOSAs at a Hot or Warm district
  • anyone whose message says they sit on the adoption or pilot committee, or lead the review
  • principals who decide for the whole school: NYC, single-school districts, charters, privates

Fast lane: anyone who isn't a teacher, at a Hot district with 5,000+ students, adopting SY26-27 or SY27-28, skips Claude.

Floor: Under $30k Total PARR, or a Starbridge score under 50, means Level 2 instead, whoever asks. No data, no floor.

SignalEffect
Message says evaluating, previewing, piloting, or asks for pricingWhy-now: teacher or coach → L2; decision maker or champion → L3
Message reveals a student, parent, vendor or nonsenseLevel 0, whatever role was picked
Adoption answer SY26-27 or SY27-28Counts only from decision makers and champions
Starbridge Blocked, or just committed to another programDown one level

Tie-breakers

  • Decision maker, champion or coach torn between 2 and 3 → 3.
  • Teacher torn between 1 and 2 → 1, unless there's an evaluation signal or a hot district.
  • Teachers reach Level 3 only through committee membership or leading the review.
  • Every decision carries a one-line reason naming the deciding factor; it shows in Slack and on the Salesforce Task.
