Inbound Lead Levels

Every samples request is sorted into one of four levels the moment it comes in. The level decides what the person sees next and who on our team follows up.

LevelWhoWhat happens next
Level 0Not a buyer People who will never buy a district curriculumParents, students, vendors, press, high-school-only teachers, anyone outside the US and Canada Thank-you page. Saved in Salesforce, no follow-up.
Level 1Interested teacher Teachers with no sign their district is choosing a curriculumA teacher at a quiet district who says nothing about a review Thank-you page asking them to loop in their principal. A follow-up email asks a few questions; no call.
Level 2Worth a quick call People close to the decision, but no timeline yetCoaches and TOSAs, math coordinators, district leaders who aren't choosing yet, teachers whose school is piloting Books a short call with our BDR (Luis or Neftali). The BDR gets a Slack alert and a task.
Level 3Ready to talk The person who decides, with a reason to change nowA curriculum director or superintendent adopting in the next two school years, or anyone on the adoption committee Books a meeting with the growth manager for their state. The growth manager gets a Slack alert and a task.

What moves a lead up

  • Their message says they're reviewing, piloting, or asks about pricing
  • They're on the curriculum or adoption committee
  • A district leader who is adopting in the next two school years
  • Our district data shows the district is actively shopping for math curriculum

What keeps a lead lower

  • A teacher's answer about adoption timing doesn't count on its own
  • Small districts (under $30k PARR) or a low score in our district data never go straight to a growth manager
  • The district just picked another curriculum
  • Their message shows they're a student, parent or vendor, whatever role they chose
