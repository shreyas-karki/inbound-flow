Samples Inbound Routing

What happens when someone requests Thinking Math! samples, and who on our team takes it from there.

1They request samplesName, role, school district, and when the district plans to adopt
→
2We look them upTheir role, how big and how active their district is, and their timeline
→
3We sort them into one of four groupsAutomatically, in about 2 seconds

each group gets a different next step

Level 0

Not a buyer

WhoParents, students, vendors, people outside the US
They seeA thank-you page
Our teamNothing. They're saved in Salesforce for reference.
Level 1

Interested teacher

WhoTeachers with no sign their district is choosing a new curriculum
They seeA thank-you page asking them to loop in their principal
Our teamNo call. A follow-up email asks a few questions.
Level 2

Worth a quick call

WhoCoaches, coordinators, and leaders who aren't choosing a curriculum yet
They seeOur BDR's calendar, to book a short call
Our teamThe BDR (Luis or Neftali) gets a Slack alert and a task to follow up
Level 3

Ready to talk

WhoDistrict leaders choosing a curriculum in the next two school years
They seeThe growth manager's calendar for their state
Our teamThe growth manager gets a Slack alert and a task to follow up
Levels 0 and 1 aren't offered a meeting.
They pick a time → The meeting lands on our Google Calendar → It's logged in Salesforce
Logging the meeting in Salesforce automatically is being set up with David.
Existing customers skip the groupsIf their district already works with us, they go straight to their account owner. Coming soon: this needs a Salesforce login from RevOps.
Small districts start with the BDRA district under $30k PARR, or with a low score in our district data, goes to Level 2 instead of Level 3.
